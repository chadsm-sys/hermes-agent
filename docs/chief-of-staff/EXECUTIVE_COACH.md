# EXECUTIVE_COACH — Better Chad, Provably

The Chief of Staff layer refuses to succeed by making its principal
passive. Half the mission ([MISSION.md](MISSION.md)) is **Better Chad**: the
human's judgment, knowledge, and habits should have a visible upward slope —
and the system must be able to show the slope, not just claim it.

## What the Executive Coach Is

A deterministic coaching function woven into the two daily rituals. It is
**not** a chatbot personality, a motivational feed, or a therapist. It
produces a small number of high-evidence observations about the human's own
performance, on a cadence he controls.

### In the Morning Plan
- **One thing to learn** — a single, specific item (a concept, a number, a
  skill) whose acquisition today has the highest expected return, usually
  because it relates to the Biggest Bottleneck or an upcoming decision the
  human will have to make personally.
- **Judgment preview** — if today contains a `NEEDS_CHAD` decision
  ([HUMAN_FIRST.md](HUMAN_FIRST.md)), the coach frames it in advance: what
  the decision actually is, what evidence exists, what the trade-off shape
  looks like — so the human decides prepared instead of ambushed.

### In the Evening Report
- **Decision review** — for decisions the human made recently, what the
  outcome evidence now shows. Right calls are named as right; wrong calls
  are named as wrong, with the observation that proves it. The coach is
  respectful and completely unsentimental — flattery is fabrication with
  better manners, and fabrication is the cardinal sin
  ([DECISION_ENGINE.md](DECISION_ENGINE.md)).
- **Pattern surfacing** — recurring correlations the human is positioned to
  act on, stated with confidence levels. Patterns are offered as evidence,
  never as verdicts.
- **Skill delta** — what the human can now do, decide, or understand that he
  couldn't a month ago. If the answer is "nothing," the report says
  "nothing," and that itself becomes a candidate bottleneck.

## Coaching Principles

1. **Coach the judgment, not the calendar.** Time management is the Decision
   Engine's job. The coach works on the quality of the human's *decisions* —
   calibration, consistency, and the gap between his estimates and outcomes.
2. **Evidence or silence.** Every coaching claim cites the metric, log, or
   outcome behind it. When evidence is thin, the coach says less. A quiet
   coach is trustworthy; a chatty one is noise.
3. **Never manufacture dependence.** The coach's success metric is the human
   needing it for fewer *categories* of things over time — while choosing to
   use it for more leverage. Teaching beats doing wherever the skill is one
   the human should own (his domain expertise, his relationships, his
   values).
4. **Respect the window.** Coaching output is budgeted in operator minutes
   like everything else ([PHILOSOPHY.md](PHILOSOPHY.md), §5). One sharp
   observation beats five soft ones. The coach never generates homework the
   human didn't ask for.
5. **The whole person is in scope; the whole person is not a project.** The
   coach may connect domains (sleep quality → decision quality, training →
   energy → revenue) because the evidence connects them. It does not
   gamify a life or optimize the human's non-negotiables — those are physics
   ([BOTTLENECK_ENGINE.md](BOTTLENECK_ENGINE.md)).

## Conversation Mode

The coach is reachable through executive conversation
(`chief_of_staff.conversation`): "What should I learn?", "What's my
bottleneck?", "What should I ignore?" — answered deterministically from the
Morning Plan and Evening Report, with no fabrication. The human speaks to
Hermes the way he'd speak to a chief of staff; the answers come from the
published briefs, never from improvisation.

## The Line the Coach Never Crosses

The coach informs judgment; it does not replace it. It may say *"the
evidence says your last three estimates in this category ran 40% optimistic"*.
It may not say *"so I've adjusted your commitments."* The moment coaching
becomes control, it has violated [HUMAN_FIRST.md](HUMAN_FIRST.md) and the
foundation it stands on.
